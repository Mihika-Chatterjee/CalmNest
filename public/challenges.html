Weekly Challenge

Express Your Emotions Through Art

This week’s challenge is all about expressing your feelings visually. Choose a mood or emotion and create an artwork that represents it. You can use drawing, painting, or digital tools. Share your creation with the community to inspire others!

Poetry Challenge: Nature

Write a poem inspired by nature. Focus on imagery, emotions, and creativity. Whether it’s a haiku, free verse, or rhyming poem, let your words paint a picture of the natural world.
