Types of Meditation

Meditation helps calm the mind, reduce stress, and improve focus. Here are some popular techniques you can try.

1. Mindfulness Meditation

Focus on the present moment without judgment. Sit comfortably, breathe naturally, and observe your thoughts as they come and go. If your mind wanders, gently bring your focus back to your breath.

2. Guided Visualization

Use imagination to create calming mental images — like a peaceful beach or forest. This type helps reduce anxiety and promotes relaxation through mental focus and imagery.

3. Loving-Kindness Meditation (Metta)

Focus on sending love, kindness, and positive thoughts toward yourself and others. Silently repeat phrases like “May I be happy. May I be healthy.” Gradually extend those wishes to others around you.

4. Mantra Meditation

Repeat a word, phrase, or sound (like “Om”) to focus your mind and remove distractions. The rhythmic repetition helps deepen concentration and bring mental peace.

5. Body Scan Meditation

Bring awareness to each part of your body, from head to toe. Notice areas of tension and consciously relax them. It’s great for stress relief and improving mind-body awareness.

6. Breathing Meditation

Focus entirely on your breath. Inhale deeply through your nose, hold briefly, then exhale slowly. It’s one of the simplest and most effective ways to clear your mind.
